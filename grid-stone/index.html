01 / Noida at a glance

Why families and investors are choosing Noida.

Noida is a planned city. Its land, roads and sectors were laid out by the NOIDA authority, which makes neighbourhoods easier to compare and titles easier to check than in older parts of NCR.

Planned sectors

Each sector has a fixed use: residential, commercial, institutional or green. You know what will be built next door.

Expressways and metro

The Noida–Greater Noida Expressway, DND Flyway and Yamuna Expressway connect to Delhi. The Aqua and Blue metro lines cover most residential belts.

Jewar airport

Noida International Airport on the Yamuna Expressway is bringing new jobs, roads and housing to the southern corridor.

Space and greenery

Newer sectors such as 150 were planned with large green areas and sports facilities, and projects here offer fewer homes per acre.

05 / Buying guide

What a Noida home really costs, and what to ask.

The listed price is only part of the total. Use this list when you compare quotes from different projects.

Costs on top of the base price

GST5%

Applies to under-construction homes. Ready homes with a completion certificate do not attract GST.

Stamp duty and registrationState rates

Paid to the UP government at registry. Ask us for the current rate for your unit and buyer profile.

Preferential location chargesVaries

Extra for park-facing, corner or higher floors. Check whether they are in the quote.

Parking and club membershipVaries

Some developers include them, others charge separately.

Maintenance depositAt possession

Usually one to two years of maintenance paid in advance at handover.

Common questions

How do I check if a project is RERA registered?

Search the project name or RERA number on up-rera.in. Each phase has its own number, registered possession date and quarterly progress reports.

Is Noida property freehold or leasehold?

Most land in Noida, Greater Noida and YEIDA is leased from the respective authority for 90 years. Buyers get a sub-lease deed at registry. Ask whether the developer’s land dues to the authority are clear.

Carpet area or super area: which should I compare?

Compare RERA carpet area, the usable floor inside your walls. Super area adds common spaces and varies a lot between projects.

Do you charge buyers a fee?

No. As an authorised channel partner, we are paid by the developer. The price you pay is the developer’s price.

Can NRIs buy through you?

Yes. We share documents and video walkthroughs on WhatsApp and help with home loans for NRIs and power of attorney for registry.
