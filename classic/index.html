How a purchase works with us

Most clients go from first call to registry in six to ten weeks.

  1. Shortlist

    You give us the brief. We come back with three to five options that actually match, with honest notes on each.

  2. Site visits

    We take you to every shortlisted property at your pace and tell you what we'd worry about.

  3. Negotiate

    We handle the back-and-forth with the seller or developer and get the number to where it should be.

  4. Paperwork

    Title check, agreement, loan coordination if needed, and registry at the sub-registrar's office.

A small team that shows up

Crestway Realty is a small advisory team working from our office in Sector 63, Noida. We work in the premium band, from ₹1.5 crore up, and only in micro-markets we visit every week.

That focus is the whole point. We don't list everything; we list what we'd be comfortable recommending to a friend, and we say plainly when a property isn't worth your time.

Buyers get a shortlist, site visits, negotiation and paperwork. Sellers get a realistic price, serious buyers only, and a quiet sale. NRIs get the same service over WhatsApp, video and a single power of attorney.
