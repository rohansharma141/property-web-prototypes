I · Why Noida

A planned city, built around roads, rail and an airport.

Noida was laid out by a development authority, sector by sector, which is why it has wide roads, green belts and clear land titles in a way older parts of NCR often don’t. Four things drive demand for homes here today.

Airport

Noida International Airport, Jewar

The new airport on the Yamuna Expressway puts the southern sectors and YEIDA on the map for buyers and businesses who want to be close to it.

Connectivity

Expressways and the Aqua Line

The Noida–Greater Noida Expressway, DND Flyway and Yamuna Expressway link the city to Delhi and Agra. The Aqua Line metro runs along the Expressway sectors.

Jobs

IT and corporate offices

Sector 62 and the Expressway office belt host large technology and services employers, which keeps rental demand steady in nearby sectors.

Living

Low density and green cover

Sectors such as 150 were planned with large open areas and sports facilities. Newer projects tend to offer fewer homes per acre than older NCR stock.

IV · Buying guide

What to check before you book in Noida.

Noida has its own rules on land, approvals and transfers. These are the questions we go through with every buyer before a booking amount changes hands.

Talk it through with us

  1. UP RERA registration

    Every project and phase should have its own number on up-rera.in. Check the registered possession date and the quarterly progress the developer has filed.

  2. Which authority, and lease terms

    Land in Noida, Greater Noida and YEIDA is leasehold from the respective authority. Ask whether the developer’s land dues are clear, as they affect registry timelines.

  3. Carpet area, not just super area

    Prices are often quoted on super area. Ask for the RERA carpet area of the exact unit so you can compare projects fairly.

  4. Payment plan and loan approval

    Compare construction-linked and possession-linked plans, and confirm which banks have approved the project for home loans.

  5. Developer track record

    Look at how the developer delivered its last two or three Noida projects, and visit one if you can.
